Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

ThatGirl75 ("the app", "we") is an iOS app published by Bruno Schuermans. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: your challenge data lives on your phone, we do not run advertising or analytics, and we only receive your name and email if you choose to sign in.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your iPhone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your name, the challenge you chose, your daily tasks, water count, notes and completion history.
  • Photos you attach to tasks, post to a challenge chat or set as your avatar, and the signature you draw on day one.
  • Your invite code, friends you have added and the posts shown in chats.
  • Your onboarding answers, such as your motivation, rhythm and struggles, which are used only to suggest a program.

This information is not uploaded to our servers. It is included in your iPhone backup according to your own iCloud or iTunes backup settings. You can erase everything at any time from Profile → Settings → Erase all data, or by deleting the app.

Signing in

Signing in is optional. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google, the provider sends the app a user identifier, your email address and, the first time, your name. The app stores these on your device to label your account and to prefill your name. We do not receive your password, and we do not share this information with anyone. Google sign-in uses Google's standard OAuth flow in a secure web view provided by iOS. Google's handling of your account is covered by Google's privacy policy. If you sign in with email, only the address you type is stored, on your device.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for camera access when you take a proof photo and photo library access when you pick one. Photos are saved inside the app's private storage on your device. The app never reads other photos from your library.

Subscriptions and payments

ThatGirl75 Plus is sold through Apple's App Store using in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment. We never see your card details. The app receives only a receipt that confirms your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Links and sharing

When you share an invite, the app creates a link and hands it to the iOS share sheet. What you do with that link is up to you. Links to this website are opened in your browser.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your data is stored on your device, you are in control of it. You can view, change or delete it inside the app. If you have signed in and want the name and email removed, sign out in Profile → Settings, or erase all data. For any question, or to exercise rights under the GDPR, email brunoschuermansok@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with an updated date.
